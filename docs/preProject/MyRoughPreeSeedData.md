## One recommendation that I think will change everything

Instead of thinking of yourself as a **.NET developer learning AI**, become an **AI systems engineer with deep backend expertise**.

Your experience with Kafka, event-driven systems, AWS, microservices, and production software is already uncommon. If you combine that with modern AI engineering (agents, MCP, RAG, evaluations, observability, tool calling) and demonstrate it through one or two exceptional public projects, you'll stand out far more than someone who has only built generic AI chatbots.

I also have one suggestion: treat this as a serious 6-month program with measurable milestones. We can design it almost like a personalized curriculum—with weekly tasks, monthly goals, GitHub issues, reading lists, project architecture, interview preparation, and content planning. By the end, you'll have not just knowledge, but a portfolio and online presence that supports a move toward senior backend or AI platform engineering roles.

I think this is absolutely achievable within your available time if we keep the scope disciplined and make every hour produce something reusable.
